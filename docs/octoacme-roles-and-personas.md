# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Designer / UX Researcher

### Role Summary
Product Designers and UX Researchers translate user needs into intuitive experiences and validate that the product delivers value in ways that are usable and understandable.

### Responsibilities
- Research customer and user needs, pain points, and workflows
- Define user journeys, interaction flows, and interface requirements
- Collaborate with Product Managers on prioritization and desired outcomes
- Partner with Developers on feasibility, edge cases, and implementation trade-offs
- Work with QA/Testing to validate usability and acceptance from a user experience perspective

### Goals
- Improve product usability, clarity, and satisfaction
- Ensure features support customer outcomes, not just delivery milestones
- Reduce rework caused by unclear experience requirements

### Typical Communication
- Discovery interviews and user feedback reviews
- Product requirement discussions and design reviews
- UX validation and cross-functional planning sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: aligns experience decisions to customer value and roadmap priorities
- With Developers: provides direction on interaction design and implementation constraints
- With QA/Testing: supports experience validation and acceptance criteria refinement
- With Stakeholders: represents user perspective and feedback in key decisions

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects shape technical direction, guide design decisions, and help teams reduce delivery risk through sound architecture and dependency management.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards, patterns, and integration approach
- Review architecture, design trade-offs, and technical dependencies
- Help break down complex work into manageable implementation steps
- Identify risks, scalability concerns, and operational impacts early
- Support Developers during design and implementation reviews

### Goals
- Balance speed, maintainability, and technical quality
- Reduce avoidable delivery risk and rework
- Maintain alignment between business goals and technical execution

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and technical design reviews
- Dependency planning and risk assessments
- Cross-team coordination for integrations and platform decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: contributes to sequencing, risk tracking, and dependency planning
- With Product Managers: helps evaluate feasibility, scope trade-offs, and technical constraints
- With Developers: provides design guidance, technical review, and decision support
- With Stakeholders: explains technical implications of roadmap and implementation choices

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify needs, shape requirements, and bridge the gap between stakeholder intent and delivery outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate business objectives into clear requirements and workflows
- Define measurable acceptance criteria and operational constraints
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and stakeholder conflicts
- Support process improvement and standardization across project work
- Help validate that planned work solves the original problem

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and reduce ambiguity
- Support better decision-making through structured analysis
- Ensure delivery matches stakeholder intent and business value

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews, workshops, and requirements discussions
- Functional specification reviews and backlog refinement
- Cross-functional alignment meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: helps shape problem statements, scope, and success criteria
- With Project Managers: supports timelines, dependencies, and impact analysis
- With Developers: clarifies functional expectations and acceptance criteria
- With QA/Testing: helps translate business needs into verifiable scenarios
- With Stakeholders: gathers input and confirms interpretation of requirements

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define how success is measured, how metrics are tracked, and how data-informed decisions are made during delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation, dashboarding, and monitoring needs
- Establish success metrics and reporting expectations
- Support experimentation and measurement planning
- Ensure data quality, privacy, and reporting accuracy
- Partner with Product Managers and stakeholders to interpret outcomes

### Goals
- Make outcomes visible and measurable
- Improve decision quality with evidence
- Connect delivery decisions to customer and business impact

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews, dashboard walkthroughs, and reporting syncs
- Measurement planning and governance discussions
- Analysis and impact interpretation with stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: aligns success metrics and outcomes to product goals
- With Developers: ensures implementation supports measurement and observability
- With Project Managers: provides data points for status and risk assessment
- With Stakeholders: explains measures and business impact clearly
- With QA/Testing: validates that business and technical outcomes are measurable

---

## Security / Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners assess and reduce risks around data handling, compliance, and system protection while enabling delivery without unnecessary delay.

### Responsibilities
- Review features for security, privacy, and compliance impact
- Define security requirements, controls, and escalation paths
- Support threat modeling and risk mitigation planning
- Validate that release decisions account for security and data protection
- Coordinate with incident and risk processes when needed

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk
- Support compliant and trustworthy delivery
- Prevent late-stage issues that slow release or create business risk

### Typical Communication
- Design review checkpoints and security approvals
- Risk triage and incident escalation discussions
- Compliance and operational readiness reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: helps assess risk and readiness for milestones and release decisions
- With Technical Leads: supports architecture review and control design
- With Developers: reviews implementation decisions and security requirements
- With QA/Testing: validates controls and security-focused acceptance criteria
- With Stakeholders: communicates risk context and mitigation status

---

## Operations / SRE Lead

### Role Summary
Operations and SRE Leads ensure that product changes are reliable, observable, and supportable across environments and customer-facing workloads.

### Responsibilities
- Define reliability, monitoring, and alerting requirements
- Review deployment readiness and operational continuity
- Support incident response and post-incident learning
- Help establish rollback and escalation processes
- Partner with engineering teams on system health and performance

### Goals
- Improve system resilience and release confidence
- Reduce operational surprises after deployment
- Ensure the product is sustainable to operate and support

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and incident coordination
- Reliability and performance discussions
- Operational feedback loops during release and post-release monitoring

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: aligns readiness, risk, and release planning
- With Developers: supports observability, deployment practices, and incident response
- With QA/Testing: validates operational checks and smoke tests
- With Stakeholders: communicates operational impact, readiness, and mitigation plans

---

## Customer Support / Success Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success representatives bring real-world customer experience and supportability considerations into the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Surface common customer pain points and support issues
- Help document launch readiness, onboarding guidance, and support materials
- Identify post-release feedback and escalation patterns
- Align product changes with customer expectations and support workflows
- Contribute to customer communications around readiness and impact

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and adoption
- Reduce avoidable support burden and dissatisfaction
- Ensure product changes are customer-ready and understandable

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback review, support issue triage, and launch readiness discussions
- Customer-facing communication and readiness planning
- Feedback loops from support and success teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: shares customer signals and priorities
- With Project Managers: supports launch readiness and communication planning
- With Developers: helps prioritize usability and supportability improvements
- With Stakeholders: brings customer impact context and readiness concerns into decisions

## Role Interactions and Team Design

These roles are not intended to be mandatory for every project. In smaller teams, one person may cover more than one role, while larger initiatives may need separate ownership for product discovery, technical direction, quality, security, and operations. The goal is not to add bureaucracy, but to make responsibilities explicit so delivery, risk, and accountability remain clear.

A strong team structure often includes:
- Product Manager + Product Designer / UX Researcher for customer value and experience
- Project Manager + Technical Lead / Architect for delivery planning and technical leadership
- Developers + QA/Testing for implementation and validation
- Security / Privacy Partner + Operations / SRE Lead for risk and reliability readiness
- Business Analyst + Customer Support / Success Representative to connect stakeholder intent with real-world execution

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a project is smaller or more focused, combine roles instead of creating artificial silos, while keeping the responsibilities and ownership explicit.
